AI-Powered Pneumonia Detection

Our system uses advanced artificial intelligence to analyze chest X-rays and detect pneumonia in children with high accuracy.

Pneumonia is a leading cause of death in children under 5 years old worldwide. Early and accurate diagnosis is crucial for effective treatment and improved outcomes.

This system helps healthcare professionals by:

  • Providing quick preliminary analysis of chest X-rays
  • Highlighting potential pneumonia cases for priority review
  • Maintaining comprehensive patient records and history
  • Generating detailed medical reports
